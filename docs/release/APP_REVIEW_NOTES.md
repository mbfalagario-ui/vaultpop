# App Review Notes Draft

VaultPop is an offline-capable arcade puzzle game. No account or demo login is required. From Home, tap Start and choose Classic Mode, Daily Vault, or Streak Mode. The Shop is available from Home, and Restore Purchases is available from both Shop and Settings.

VaultPop includes optional in-app purchases for fictional in-game Vault Coins, fixed booster packs, a permanent ad-free upgrade, and an optional monthly VaultPass Plus subscription. Vault Coins and boosters are used only inside the arcade puzzle game, have no real-world value, cannot be exchanged, cannot be withdrawn, cannot be transferred, and cannot be converted into cryptocurrency. The app does not include real cryptocurrency, mining, wallets, trading, staking, NFTs, cash-out, gambling, sweepstakes, or real-world rewards. Restore Purchases is available from the Shop and Settings screens.

Products:

- `app.vaultpop.coins.small`: fixed 1,000 Vault Coins.
- `app.vaultpop.coins.medium`: fixed 5,500 Vault Coins.
- `app.vaultpop.coins.large`: fixed 12,000 Vault Coins.
- `app.vaultpop.boosters.starter`: fixed 5 Bonus Lives, 5 Chain Boosts, and 3 Vault Bursts.
- `app.vaultpop.remove_ads`: permanent removal of every ad placement and rewarded prompt.
- `app.vaultpop.vaultpass.monthly`: monthly ad-free access, premium theme, priority support routing, and a fixed monthly booster grant.

Purchases are verified against Apple-signed transaction data before delivery. Consumables use a unique transaction ledger and are not restored. Cancelled, failed, pending, unverified, or revoked transactions grant nothing.

AdMob banners appear only on Home, Mode Select, Shop, Cosmetics, Support, and Results. Interstitials are limited to natural post-round breaks, rewarded ads are user-initiated, and app-open ads require a prior completed round plus a background return. No ad appears on first launch or during gameplay. Ads use contextual requests by default and the app does not request ATT.

The private Support screen accepts a category, message, and optional email. It includes the disclosed anonymous install ID, app/build version, device model, and priority-routing flag. If secure ticket delivery is unavailable, the app opens a prefilled support email.
